Meeting Room Rental in Taipei
Xinyi District & World Trade Center

Need a professional, quiet place to meet a client, run an interview or hold a workshop? Morning Stars offers hourly meeting rooms at two locations on Keelung Road, Taipei — both a short walk from the MRT. Each room seats 6–8 people. Book online, pay by the hour, far more professional than a cafe.

Home / Meeting Room Rental

Quick answer: Meeting rooms at both Morning Stars locations cost NT$400 per hour (about US$13). Minimum booking 1 hour, maximum 3 hours per session. Open to the public — you do not need to be a tenant. English-speaking assistance available via LINE.

🕐 Off-peak offer: Weekdays before 12:00 (both locations) and 18:00–21:00 at the World Trade branch — only NT$300 per hour. An off-peak package (10 hours, NT$2,800, prepaid, valid 3 months) is also available on request. Rooms are for business and adult activities only; not available for children's classes or childcare use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a meeting room?
Use the online booking calendar on our website, or contact us via LINE @mstars or by phone at 0903-368-856. We can handle the whole booking in English.
Can non-tenants rent a meeting room?
Yes. Our meeting rooms are open to the public. For non-tenants the booking becomes final once payment has been received.
How much does it cost?
NT$400 per hour at both locations, with a one-hour minimum and a three-hour maximum per session.
Do you have English-speaking staff?
We can assist you in English via LINE and email, and our staff will help you on site. For detailed contract discussions we recommend messaging us in advance so we can prepare.
How do I get there?
The WTC branch is a 5-minute walk from Taipei 101 / World Trade Center MRT Station (Red Line). The Xinyi branch is a 6-minute walk from Liuzhangli MRT Station (Brown Line).
Can I use the meeting room as a long-term office?
We do not recommend it. Meeting rooms suit short, formal sessions. For daily work, consider a private office or a dedicated desk.
