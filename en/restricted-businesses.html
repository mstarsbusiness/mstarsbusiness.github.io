Eligibility Guide

Which Businesses Cannot Use
a Virtual Office Address in Taiwan?

Check your business type before you sign — and what to do if a registration-only address is not enough.

Home / Virtual Office / Restricted Businesses

Quick answer: At Morning Stars, a registration-only address cannot be used by international trading companies (especially those that need export VAT refunds), food businesses, environmental-service businesses, or any business that needs a physical work or activity site — such as warehousing and packing, sports training or sports venues. Licensed professions that require a fixed workplace usually need a dedicated desk or private office instead.

Why some businesses are not accepted

A registration address is used for official records and mail. The tax office may visit to confirm what a business actually does. If the business needs goods, kitchens, equipment or a training floor on site, a shared registration address cannot support it, and the registration is likely to be rejected or questioned later.

Not accepted at our addresses

  • International trading (export / import-export), especially companies that need export VAT refunds
  • Food businesses — making, processing, wholesaling, retailing, importing or selling food online, including restaurants, drinks, bakery products and health foods
  • Environmental services — waste collection and treatment, recycling, environmental engineering or equipment
  • Businesses that need a physical site — warehousing and packing, sports training, sports and leisure venues
  • Construction contractors and other trades that require a work site or full-time licensed staff
  • Small businesses applying to be exempt from issuing uniform invoices, because the tax office needs to inspect the actual premises

Reviewed case by case

Pure import businesses, real-estate development or trading, online retail without a store, and some licensed trades may be possible depending on the details. Ask us before you sign.

Licensed professions

Professions that require a fixed workplace, such as an insurance brokerage, cannot use a registration-only address. They can usually register with a dedicated desk or private office at the same location. When you move out, the company must change its registered address before the lease ends.

For foreign founders, see also how to get a company registration address in Taipei. Source: Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act, Laws & Regulations Database of the Republic of China (Taiwan).
