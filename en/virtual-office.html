Important: Not Every Business Type Qualifies

Under Taiwanese tax office rules, some business activities cannot be registered at a virtual office address — for example, businesses requiring physical premises (restaurants, clinics, manufacturing), and international trading (export / import-export) is not permitted at our addresses — in particular, companies that need export VAT refunds cannot register here, and tax refunds cannot be processed through this address. Pure import trading may be accepted subject to conditions.

Tell us your intended business scope before you sign and we will confirm whether it can be registered. This is practical guidance, not tax or legal advice — the final decision rests with your accountant and the National Taxation Bureau.
